How good is the detector?

Results from the saved evaluation (reports/before_after.md) on the BigVul test set (27,720 functions, 5.2% vulnerable). The test set was opened once. Scores are model scores, not calibrated probabilities.

Accuracy is misleadingGuessing "safe" every time is right about 95% of the time, yet it finds no vulnerabilities at all.
Fine-tuning helped a littleCodeBERT went from random (PR-AUC 0.045) to above chance (0.151). The simple TF-IDF baseline is about as good, and the gap is not significant.
It does not transferOn outside data (CVEfixes) both models are at chance. The detector is weak, so treat every result as a hint for a human reviewer.
